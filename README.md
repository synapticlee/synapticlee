## Hi there 👋

I am a **Data Reporter** at [Mississippi Today](https://mississippitoday.org/). I graduated in May with a master's in investigative journalism at Columbia University. You can read my work on [my website](http://julielee.net/). 

For two years, I was the Technology for Liberty Fellow at the ACLU of Massachusetts, where I worked on issues like surveillance technologies, policing and algorithmic harms. I oversaw the [Data for Justice project](data.aclum.org/), publishing data-driven accountability stories and crafting interactive visualizations.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher, studying reinforcement learning and decision-making using computational methods. I have a PhD in neuroscience from University College London.
